Predict how many people will use the taxi service every week based on price, population, income, and parking charges.





Here is a small, crisp, easy-to-understand explanation of your dataset:

1. Priceperweek

Meaning: Cost of bus/train ticket per week.
Example: 15 = ₹15 weekly ticket.
Effect: Higher price → fewer people use transport.

2. Population

Meaning: Total number of people in the city.
Example: 1800000 = 18 lakh people.
Effect: More population → more riders.

3. Monthlyincome

Meaning: Average monthly salary of people.
Example: 5800 = ₹5800/month income.
Effect: Higher income → some people may use private vehicles instead of bus/train.

4. Averageparkingpermonth

Meaning: Monthly parking cost for private vehicles.
Example: 50 = ₹50 parking charge/month.
Effect: Higher parking cost → more people choose bus/train.

5. Numberofweeklyriders (Output)

Meaning: Total people using public transport in one week.
Example: 192000 = 1,92,000 people traveled in a week.

Simple idea:

The model predicts:

“How many people will use public transport based on ticket price, population, income, and parking cost?”
